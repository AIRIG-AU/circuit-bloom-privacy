Circuit Bloom – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AIRIG Playtime ("we", "us") handles information in the Circuit Bloom mobile game (the "App"), available on Google Play (package name: au.com.airig.circuitbloom).

Information we collect

Circuit Bloom does not collect, store, or share any personal or sensitive user data. The App does not require you to create an account or sign in, does not display advertising, does not use an advertising ID, and does not include analytics or tracking tools. Any game progress is stored only on your device.

Third-party services

The App is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information in connection with your use of Google Play and Google Play services under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive personal information from Google about you.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Data deletion

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no data held by us to delete. You can remove all locally stored game data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@airig.com.au.
